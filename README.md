📌 Project Overview

**FUTURE_DS_01** is a Business Sales Performance Dashboard developed as **Task 1 of the Data Science & Analytics Internship at Future Interns**. The project focuses on analyzing the **Superstore Sales dataset** to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product and category performance, regional trends, and changes in sales over time.

Using **Microsoft Power BI**, the dataset was cleaned and transformed with **Power Query**, while **DAX** was used for analytical calculations and measures. The processed data was presented through interactive visualizations, KPIs, filters, and charts to provide a clear and comprehensive view of business performance.

The project demonstrates the practical application of **data analysis, data visualization, Power BI, and business intelligence** techniques to transform raw business data into meaningful insights.


🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Monitor key business performance indicators.
* Identify high-performing products and categories.
* Analyze regional and time-based sales trends.
* Understand patterns in sales and profitability.
* Transform raw data into meaningful visual insights.
* Develop an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.


📂 Dataset

The project uses the **Superstore Sales Dataset**, which contains transactional information related to:

* Orders
* Customers
* Products
* Categories
* Sales
* Profit

The dataset was cleaned and prepared using **Power Query** before being used for dashboard development.
